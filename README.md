# Joseph E Walker

I develop practical Android apps with AI assistance, drawing on experience in computer setup and repair, plumbing and trucking. I focus on what a tool needs to do, whether its workflow makes sense, and whether its results hold up against concrete examples.

## Background and approach

My paid technical background includes computer setup, Ethernet installation, router/hub networking, driver installation, component replacement and monitor repair at Fun Time Arcade in 2008–2009. Plumbing and trucking work also involved diagnosing practical problems, following procedures and keeping useful records.

I am interested in functional software testing, practical technical evaluation and reviewing technical instructions and AI-assisted outputs for correctness and usability. My app work is self-directed and AI-assisted; project languages are not a claim that I wrote every line independently or have held a software-engineering job.
